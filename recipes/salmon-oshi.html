Salmon Oshi

Description

A delicious sushi made of blow torched salmon on top of rice.

Ingredients

  • Salmon
  • Rice
  • Green onion
  • Sauce

Steps

  1. Slice the salmon into pieces, rectangular.
  2. Season the rice with vinegar and shape appropriately
  3. Place salmon atop rice. Blow torch/mildly cook.
  4. Garnish
