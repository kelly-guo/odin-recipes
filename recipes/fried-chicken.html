Fried Chicken

Description

A cripsy, delightful battered chicken recipe. Savory and satiating.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Eggs
  • Seasonings
  • Chicken breast
  • Corn flakes
  • Sauce

Steps

  1. Mix flour and seasonings together in a bowl
  2. Crack eggs into separate bowl
  3. Cut chicken into appropriate size then dip into eggs, and coat in flour
  4. Repeat previous step again for double coating
  5. Heat oil up then fry until golden brown
  6. Enjoy with sauce of choice
